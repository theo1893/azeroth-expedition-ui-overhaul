## 从项目基线转译出的核心描述

后续英文提示词不是对用户口述的直译，而是把项目基线收敛为以下约束：

- 它首先是屏幕左下角一本正在使用的战地旧书，其次才是一组 UI 边框。
- 纸面承担信息，深棕旧皮革建立实体边界，暗哑氧化黄铜只作次要保护件。
- 厚封皮、两至四层毛边书页、皮革索引签和轻微不对称属于不可变特征。
- 保留经典魔兽二维手绘与低分辨率时代的材质分块，不做现代面板、网页
  标签、规则工业网格或摄影级产品渲染。
- pfUI 的真实结构和操作习惯优先；美术资源不得预先画死 Tab、按钮、
  输入框、状态栏或滚动条。

## 提示词 A：主书框母版

Create a production-ready, modular 2D hand-painted UI asset for World of
Warcraft 1.18.1 Turtle WoW, following the project's locked “Azeroth Expedition
Journal / Battlefield Old Book V1” art baseline.

The project's authoritative design description is this: the chat window is a
physical old battlefield book in active use at the lower-left of the game
screen, not a vintage-looking panel laid over the world. Paper is the
information-bearing surface, worn dark-brown leather defines the physical
boundary, and muted oxidized brass is only a secondary protective material.
Vanilla structure and pfUI behavior take priority over added ornament.

Design only the empty base frame, viewed straight from the front. It must
unmistakably read as a compact, heavily used book: a worn dark-brown leather
cover, two to four visible layers of irregular deckled parchment pages, warm
ochre smoked parchment in the reading area, subtle binding compression and
page-stack shadows, and restrained oxidized-brass corner protection. Preserve
the slightly asymmetric, hand-drawn Vanilla World of Warcraft character of the
locked baseline and approved earlier version. Keep the decoration compact so
the book does not block unnecessary game space. The material should feel
painted for a 2004-era fantasy game UI, with clear low-resolution-era material
grouping, not photorealistic and not like a modern product render.

This is a modular runtime asset, not a screenshot and not a finished chat
window. Do not include tabs, tab slots, labels, text, buttons, side tool slots,
scroll bars, input fields, status fields, icons, dragons, crests, gems, runes,
or any fixed control geometry. Keep the entire central parchment area quiet,
continuous, and readable.

Technical composition requirements:

- orthographic front view with no perspective;
- true transparent background;
- one complete frame centered with generous transparent padding;
- source resolution at least 2048 × 1536;
- all four corners may have local handmade asymmetry, but the horizontal and
  vertical join lines for a nine-slice must be perfectly aligned;
- top and bottom edges must each share one stable baseline without steps;
- corner ornaments may protrude only into transparent outer padding;
- the center parchment must be suitable for tiling or controlled stretching;
- low-contrast wear concentrated at page edges and binding, not in the text
  area;
- parchment and leather visually dominate; oxidized brass remains secondary.

Reject modern flat UI, web tabs, rigid geometric grids, perfectly mirrored
industrial construction, glossy gold, black-and-red Diablo framing,
Elder-Scrolls-like minimalist text overlays, giant dragon heads, spires,
skulls, neon glow, large runes, fixed button slots, photorealistic wood, and a
polished furniture-panel appearance.

Among the supplied references, Image 1 is the preferred earlier material and
wear reference, but remove its baked tab slots and side controls. Image 2 is
only an in-game scale and integration reference; do not copy its fixed control
layout. Image 3 may be used only for page-edge and hand-painted material
detail; ignore its dragon, spires, heavy metal architecture, and fixed slots.

## 提示词 B：Tab 承托带与状态

Create a modular sprite source for the chat tabs of the same locked
“Azeroth Expedition Journal / Battlefield Old Book V1” UI.

The tabs must look like small worn leather index markers inserted into the top
edge of a thick old book, not metal plaques and not website tabs. Match the
dark leather, warm parchment, muted oxidized brass, irregular hand-painted
edges, stitching, and restrained wear of the approved earlier chat frame.

Produce:

1. one continuous horizontal leather/page-layer shelf with no vertical
   dividers and no fixed slots;
2. four text-free versions of one tab: normal, hover, selected, and disabled.

Every tab state must use the same transparent canvas, width, bottom baseline,
and left/right cap geometry. Normal is recessed and low contrast. Hover adds
only a slight candle-gold edge. Selected rises visually by a small amount
inside the same canvas and becomes warmer. Disabled is desaturated and dim.
Keep a broad undecorated center region so live Chinese or English text can be
rendered by pfUI. Design the tab so its left cap, stretchable center, and right
cap can be separated without stretching rivets or edge wear.

Use an orthographic front view, true transparent background, no cast shadow
outside the alpha boundary, no text, no symbols, no numbering, no fixed labels,
no glossy gold, no rigid brass frames, no web-style geometry, and no modern
notification dots.

## 提示词 C：输入区、底栏与未读提示

Create a modular control sprite source for the same locked Battlefield Old
Book chat frame.

Produce three coordinated, text-free elements:

1. a parchment writing strip for the chat input field in normal and focused
   states;
2. a compact reusable leather/parchment field for each of pfUI's three bottom
   status segments;
3. a small dark-purple-red wax seal or cloth-knot unread indicator.

The input strip must feel like a shallow ink-writing area pressed into the
book page, never a black rectangular edit box. Focus adds only a slightly
deeper ink line or restrained warm edge. The bottom field must support
left-cap, stretchable-center, and right-cap slicing and must not be baked into
the book frame. The unread indicator must be subtle, transparent-backed, and
clearly pre-modern, with no red notification dot or number badge.

Match the approved Vanilla Warcraft hand-painted old leather, smoked
parchment, muted brass, and low-resolution material language. Use orthographic
front views, true transparency, aligned baselines, generous extraction
spacing, no text, no icons, no runes, no glow, no rigid grid, and no
photorealistic product-render finish.
