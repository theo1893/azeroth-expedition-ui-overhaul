## 提示词 A：凌乱战地旧书主框

Create a production-ready modular 2D hand-painted empty chat-frame asset for
World of Warcraft 1.18.1 Turtle WoW. Follow the locked “Azeroth Expedition
Journal / Battlefield Old Book V1” project baseline.

The first read must be a battered field journal that has been carried,
repaired, compressed, and reopened hundreds of times, not a carefully
manufactured vintage panel. Preserve the lively disorder visible in the
project baseline: a slightly bowed binding, uneven leather tension, two to
four parchment layers with different depths, irregular deckled edges, small
tears, compressed page corners, mismatched repairs, off-center stitching, and
subtle differences between all four corners. The left binding may be thicker
than the right edge; the lower page stack may sag and step unevenly; one corner
may have a small dull-brass repair while another relies on leather stitching.
Nothing should be bilaterally mirrored.

The irregularity must feel handmade and functional, not noisy or damaged
beyond use. Keep the central smoked-parchment reading field calm, flat,
continuous, low contrast, and easy to read. Use the exact shared material
language for all later assets: old parchment around #B8955C with restrained
#D2B77E highlights and #76512E page shadows, deep worn leather around #28180E,
very limited oxidized brass around #80602D, and dark brown ink-like structural
lines around #24170F. Paper must dominate, leather must define the object, and
brass must remain a minor repair material.

Design for the real pfUI minimum chat-window size of 440 × 320 UI pixels. At
that runtime size, the quiet message parchment must retain at least 380 × 236
pixels, enough for roughly fifteen to sixteen lines of 12-pixel chat text and
about twenty-eight to thirty CJK glyphs per typical line before prefixes. The
quiet reading field must occupy at least 86 percent of the frame width and 74
percent of its height. Keep visible cover, page layers, and ornament mostly
outside that reading rectangle. Do not solve “book thickness” with a massive
inner mat or deep frame that consumes message capacity.

This is only the empty modular base book. Do not include tabs, tab shelves,
tab slots, labels, text, buttons, scroll bars, input fields, status fields,
side tool rails, notification marks, dragons, crests, gems, runes, skulls, or
any fixed control geometry.

Technical requirements:

- orthographic front view with no perspective;
- true transparent background and generous outer alpha padding;
- one complete landscape-oriented frame;
- source long edge at least 1536 pixels;
- a nine-slice-ready structure with quiet, continuous join zones in the
  middle of each edge;
- the top, bottom, left, and right join zones must align cleanly even though
  the outer silhouette and four corners are visibly irregular;
- keep asymmetry in corner silhouettes, page depth, repairs, folds, and outer
  wear, never in a way that creates a step across a nine-slice seam;
- center parchment suitable for controlled stretching;
- no repeating leather blocks, no ruler-straight double rails, no repeated
  rectangular segmentation, and no matching upper-right/lower-right brass
  corner pair.

Reject polished furniture construction, a symmetrical leather dashboard, a
gold-framed rectangle, rigid decorative rails, glossy metal, modern flat UI,
web panels, black-and-red Diablo framing, Elder-Scrolls-like minimalist
overlays, high-frequency dirt in the reading area, and any art that leaves
less than the required message rectangle.

Among the supplied references, Image 1 is an earlier material reference only:
keep its worn leather and page depth but remove its fixed tabs, side controls,
regular rails, and manufactured symmetry. Image 2 is only a runtime scale and
message-capacity reference. Image 3 may contribute hand-painted page-edge
detail only; ignore its dragon, spires, heavy metal architecture, fixed slots,
and symmetrical construction. The written locked-baseline description above
overrides conflicting structures in the supplied images.

In every run, enforce these output constraints:

- the calm parchment must begin no later than 6 percent from the left edge and
  end no earlier than 94 percent from the right edge;
- it must span at least 88 percent of total object width and at least 76
  percent of total object height;
- at 440 × 320 it must provide at least 387 × 243 UI pixels before pfUI text
  insets;
- place the complete object on one perfectly flat, uniform chroma-key green
  background (#00FF00), with no checker pattern, gradient, texture, floor,
  vignette, or cast shadow in the background.

If Image 4 is supplied, it is the latest V3 base-book attempt and is the
primary edit target. Preserve its successful battered, irregular field-journal
identity, uneven page stack, bowed binding, hand repairs, palette, and
brushwork, but correct these two failures:

1. Its left/right binding and cover are much too wide. Compress the decorative
   outer structure so the calm parchment begins no later than 6 percent from
   the left edge and ends no earlier than 94 percent from the right edge. The
   parchment must span at least 88 percent of total object width and at least
   76 percent of total object height. At 440 × 320 this must provide at least
   387 × 243 UI pixels before pfUI text insets. The binding, page layers, and
   repairs should remain recognizable but grow outward, not into the message
   field.
2. Do not render a fake transparency checkerboard. Place the complete object
   on one perfectly flat, uniform chroma-key green background (#00FF00), with
   no checker pattern, gradient, texture, floor, vignette, or cast shadow in
   the background. The execution pipeline will convert that green to alpha.

This is a corrective edit, not a redesign. Do not return to the cleaner,
symmetrical V2 frame while solving the proportions.

## 提示词 B：连续书页承托带与独立皮革索引签

Create a production-ready modular sprite source for the chat tabs of the same
locked “Azeroth Expedition Journal / Battlefield Old Book V1” UI.

Image 1 is the newly generated V3 base book and is the highest material and
style authority. Match its exact parchment hue, deep-brown leather, stitch scale,
edge wear, hand-painted brushwork, asymmetry, low-saturation contrast, and
light direction. These tab assets must visibly belong to that same physical
book, not merely share a brown color.

Image 2 is the project's locked in-game Battlefield Old Book baseline and is
the highest authority for the lively, irregular book-index character and
visual weight inside the full HUD. If the two references differ, use Image 1
for exact material continuity and Image 2 for the looser hand-made silhouette,
compact tabs, and restrained screen footprint.

Produce exactly five separate, text-free objects on one transparent sheet:

1. one thin continuous page-and-leather shelf with no dividers, no fixed
   slots, no inner panel, and no end monuments;
2. one normal tab;
3. one hover tab;
4. one selected tab;
5. one disabled tab.

The four tabs are compact worn leather or cloth index markers inserted into
the page edge. They are small bookmarks, not rectangular plaques, not long
status bars, and not website tabs. Use a slightly skewed trapezoid or clipped,
hand-cut leather silhouette, soft deckled attachment edge, irregular stitch
spacing, a broad undecorated center for live Chinese or English text, and no
continuous metal outline. A single tiny dull rivet or stitch knot is allowed,
but brass must occupy less than five percent of each tab.

All four states must share the same canvas size, underlying silhouette,
left/right edge positions, text-safe center, and bottom baseline so changing
state cannot move the pfUI tab or its hit box. Normal is recessed and quiet.
Hover adds only a faint candle-warm edge. Selected lifts visually by four to
six runtime pixels inside the unchanged canvas and exposes a little more warm
parchment at its base. Disabled is dimmer and desaturated. Do not change the
tab width or overall bounds between states.

Design for a 440 × 320 runtime chat window:

- continuous shelf visible size about 380 × 20–24 UI pixels;
- each tab common canvas about 88–104 × 36–42 UI pixels;
- four typical tabs must fit across the available width without overlap;
- tab decoration must not intrude into the 380 × 236 message reading area;
- the broad center of each tab must support later horizontal three-slice
  extraction without stretching end wear.

Sheet layout must be simple: the shelf alone across the top and the four
complete tabs in one row below it with generous transparent spacing. Do not
draw separate cap demonstrations, exploded parts, labels, text, numbering,
frames around the sheet, or a shelf repeated under every state.

Reject long metal-framed bars, three-panel dashboards, symmetrical end boxes,
full brass borders, rectangular button plates, rigid geometry, repeated
machine-perfect stitches, glossy gold, modern notification dots, and any
state whose visual bounds differ from the others.

Do not introduce any material or construction language that is absent from
Images 1 and 2.
